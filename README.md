## Research & Scientific Projects

### Tiling Optimization (Polyominoes) - [link](https://github.com/KarlGarridoAndersson/2DTilingOptimization)
*Discrete optimization of geometric compactness through heuristic assembly methods.* 

* **Problem statement:** Formulation of an **NP-complete** tiling problem.
* **Approach:** Development of three heuristics ($H_1, H_2, H_3$) based on block morphology.
* **Results:** Use of compactness and morphological resemblance metrics to reach dense configurations.


### Health State Prediction of Dynamical Systems - [link](https://github.com/KarlGarridoAndersson/DynamicEnvironmentModeling)
*General framework for forecasting system degradation from time series.*

* **Formalization:** Definition of a health function $H(X(t))$ to turn raw data into decision-making indicators.
* **Versatility:** Modeling approach that generalizes to finance, industry, or medicine.
* **Technical:** Analysis and prediction on multivariate time series.


### Emergent Alignment Through Cognitive Immersion - [link]()

*Proposal of a new paradigm for AI alignment based on experiential learning in complex digital environments.*

* **Problem:** Analysis of the limitations of RLHF and behavioral guardrails, highlighting the risks of proxy objective optimization and the gap between external and internal alignment.
* **Architecture:** Design of an immersion-based framework combining sensorimotor grounding, interconnected short- and long-term memory, intrinsic agency, and continuous temporal plasticity.
* **Philosophical:** Exploration of emergent norm formation, the development of persistent agent identities, and the ethical implications of autonomous AI systems.
